FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Custom Design and Template Customization?

With Custom Design we build your website from scratch, tailored to your brand. With Template Customization we adapt an existing template with your branding, copy, and photos. Both are professional and unique.

Can I combine branding with a website?

Yes. We align the branding and website process so everything feels consistent from start to finish.

Are there payment terms?

Yes. We work with a deposit at the start and the remaining amount upon delivery.
