Our Process

How We Work Together

Every project starts with a detailed form. Every project ends with a website that works. Here's what happens in between.

1

Intake Form: everything in writing

Everything starts with a detailed intake form. You fill in how your brand looks, who your audience is, what your style preference is, and what your website needs to achieve. This way I have everything in writing and can hit the ground running. Prefer a personal conversation? We can schedule a call instead. Either option works perfectly.

Right after sign-up
2

Strategy & Proposal

Based on your form (or call), I put together a clear proposal: scope, timeline, price, and approach. You know exactly what you're getting, when you'll get it, and what it costs. Once you approve, we get started immediately.

Week 1
3

Design & Development

I get to work, entirely based on your intake form and agreed strategy. For Custom Design you get 3 feedback rounds; for Template Tailored you get 2 feedback rounds. You receive progress updates so you always know where we stand.

Week 2–6
4

Review & Refinement

You review the design and share your feedback. I incorporate your notes carefully and refine until everything feels exactly as you envisioned: from the copy to the buttons to the mobile view.

Week 5–7
5

Final Approval

After the final refinement we review everything together: copy, links, mobile view, and load speed. You give your final approval. We only go live when you are 100% satisfied.

Week 7–8
6

Launch 🚀

Your website goes live. I handle the technical checks, ensure a smooth handover, and remain available for any questions in the first weeks after launch.

Week 8
